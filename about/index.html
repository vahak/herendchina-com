About This Herend China Guide

herendchina.com comes from Paris Tableware, a family-owned store in Ridgewood, New Jersey, since 1982, which sells Herend china through paristableware.com. We are a Herend retailer and wrote this guide ourselves. The Herend Porcelain Manufactory does not run or review it; the manufactory's own address is herend.com.

Who we are

Paris Tableware has sold fine tableware from the same Ridgewood store since 1982. Orders are packed by hand there and shipped across the continental United States. The store FAQ lists Herend among the brands it carries as an authorized retailer, next to other European porcelain, crystal and silver.

This site collects answers to the questions people bring to a china shop: how to tell which pattern an inherited plate belongs to, whether a teapot comes in a larger size, what a five-piece setting includes. It is organized pattern by pattern.

Where the facts come from

  • History: the manufactory's website and Wikipedia, linked at the foot of every page.
  • Counts, sizes and item numbers: the Paris Tableware catalog as it stood on October 24, 2026. Stock moves daily, so treat counts as a snapshot.
  • Names of products and patterns: copied character for character from paristableware.com, parentheses and all.
  • Search interest: Ahrefs estimates of average monthly searches in the United States, taken in October 2026.
  • Prices: left out on purpose, because they change; paristableware.com always shows the current one.

The guide pages are written and signed by Tamar Medzadourian of Paris Tableware.

Found an error? Tell us at info@paristableware.com, with the page and the problem. Corrections go up with a new date on the page.

Who pays for the site

Paris Tableware covers the cost of herendchina.com. Every shopping link points to our own store, and the store makes money on what is bought there. No brand, advertiser or partner pays for placement here, Herend sees no page before it is published, and you will not find customer reviews on this site.

Contact

To ask about an order, a Herend piece on paristableware.com, or something you read here, write to info@paristableware.com or phone (201) 652-4233. More about the store is on its About Us page, and its contact page has a message form.
